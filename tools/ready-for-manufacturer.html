Quick answer

You are ready for manufacturers when they can quote from your files without guessing.

Readiness = Σ (importance × answer) ÷ Σ (importance × 2), where Yes = 2, Partly = 1, No = 0 Critical gap = a most-important question answered No

Design files and realistic quantities carry the most weight, because suppliers cannot price accurately without them.

Method and assumptions

Each answer scores Yes 2, Partly 1, No 0, multiplied by the question's importance weight Readiness % = total score ÷ maximum possible score × 100 Gaps are ranked by weight × points missing

Assumptions

  • Importance weights reflect what manufacturers most need to quote accurately.
  • A critical gap is a top-weighted question answered No.

Limits

  • It is a self-check of your preparation; it cannot judge the quality of your files or design.
  • Manufacturers' requirements vary; ask what each one needs.

How to read your result

  • About 80% or more with no critical gaps: ready to go ahead, with the remaining gaps closed in parallel.
  • About 55 to 80%, or one critical gap: possible, but with avoidable risk. Close the top gaps first if you can.
  • Below about 55%, or several critical gaps: too early. Work through the gaps in the order listed.

The weights and thresholds are Orion Design's rules of thumb for planning, not a formal standard. Each gap links to the guide or tool that helps close it. Save your result to your product plan and repeat the check as you progress.

FAQ

When should I contact manufacturers?

When you can give them 3D CAD, drawings with critical tolerances, a bill of materials and realistic quantities. Earlier conversations can be useful for advice on process and design, but quotes before then are rough guesses.

Do I need a finished design to get a quote?

Not finished, but defined enough to price. A design that is still changing will be quoted with allowances, and the price will change when the design does.

Should I sign an NDA with a manufacturer?

It is sensible before sharing detailed designs, especially with new suppliers. It does not replace choosing trustworthy partners and sharing only what is needed.

What if I do not know my quantities?

Give your best realistic estimate for the first order and the first year, with the evidence behind it. Suppliers price very differently for 100 and 10,000 units, so a range helps.

Is anything I answer stored?

Not on a server. The check runs in your browser, and your answers are remembered in your own browser's storage so they are still there when you come back; clearing this site's data removes them. The result is added to your product plan only if you choose to save it there, and the plan also stays in your browser.
