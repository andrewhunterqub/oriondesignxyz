Quick answer

Only what is left after fees, VAT, making and shipping each reward goes towards tooling and launch costs.

Collected per backer = pledge (+ shipping if charged separately) Left per backer = collected − fees − VAT in the pledge − reward cost − shipping Backers needed = (fixed costs + campaign costs) ÷ left per backer

A campaign can hit its headline target and still leave too little to pay for tooling, especially when shipping is under-estimated or the reward is priced like a retail discount.

How to read your result

  • Comfortable surplus: the campaign covers fixed and campaign costs with room for surprises. Keep a reserve for manufacturing delays and support.
  • Small surplus or shortfall: revisit the pledge price, the reward cost, shipping or the fixed costs you expect the campaign to fund.
  • Negative per backer: each reward loses money. Raise the pledge, charge shipping, or reduce the reward cost before launching; more backers would make it worse.

Fees, VAT treatment and shipping vary by platform, country and product, so the defaults are examples: check your platform's current fees and take accounting advice on VAT. FAQ

How much should a crowdfunding reward cost?

Enough that, after platform and payment fees, VAT, making the reward and shipping it, each backer still contributes towards tooling and launch costs. Many campaigns underprice rewards as a retail-style discount and then find the money runs out before delivery.

Do I pay VAT on crowdfunding pledges?

Pledges made in return for a reward are usually treated as sales, so VAT can be due if you are VAT-registered. The rules depend on the details, so take advice from an accountant before setting reward prices.

Should I charge shipping separately?

Often, yes, especially for international backers, because shipping costs vary widely by destination. Either way, get real carrier quotes for your parcel size and weight before launch.

What fixed costs should the campaign fund?

Typically tooling, certification, the set-up costs of the first production batch and the campaign itself. Include contingency: delays and tool modifications are common in hardware.

Are the platform fee defaults accurate?

They are example values only. Platforms change their fees and payment providers charge differently by country and card type, so check the current terms of the platform you plan to use.
