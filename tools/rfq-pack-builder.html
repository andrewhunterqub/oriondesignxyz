Quick answer

A good RFQ tells every supplier the same things: what to make, how many, to what standard, delivered how, and what to include in the price.

Completeness = items ready (weighted by how much they affect the quote) + quantities stated

CAD and drawings carry the most weight because suppliers cannot price accurately without them. The generated RFQ asks for the details you need to compare quotes fairly: tooling, lead times, minimum order, payment and delivery terms.

How to use the RFQ

  1. Complete the checklist and fill the gaps it lists.
  2. Copy the RFQ text into an email or document and edit it for your product.
  3. Send the same RFQ and attachments to each shortlisted supplier, with the same reply date.
  4. Put the replies side by side on a landed-cost basis with the Landed Cost Calculator.

How to read your result

  • 85% or more: suppliers have what they need to quote accurately.
  • 60 to 85%: they can quote, but expect questions and allowances in the price.
  • Below 60%: quotes will be rough and hard to compare; prepare the missing items first.

The score is a rule of thumb for how complete the pack is, not a guarantee of a good quote. How to find and brief a manufacturer and the documents to prepare explain each item. Save your RFQ to your product plan.

FAQ

What is a request for quotation (RFQ)?

A request to suppliers to price a defined piece of work, sent with the drawings, quantities and requirements they need. Sending the same RFQ to several suppliers lets you compare their quotes fairly.

Should I tell suppliers my target price?

It often helps: a realistic target tells suppliers what you are trying to achieve and invites cost-saving suggestions. Leave it out if you have no basis for it yet, or if you want unanchored quotes first.

What should a quote include?

Unit prices at your quantities, tooling and set-up costs with ownership and tool life, lead times to samples and production, minimum order quantity, payment terms, delivery terms, and any recommended design changes. The generated RFQ asks for all of these.

Is what I type stored anywhere?

Not on a server. The RFQ is generated in your browser, and your inputs are remembered in your own browser's storage so they are still there when you come back; clearing this site's data removes them. The RFQ is added to your product plan only if you choose to save it there, and the plan also stays in your browser.

How many suppliers should I send an RFQ to?

Enough to compare, usually three to five well-chosen suppliers. Sending it to many more takes time to manage and rarely improves the result.
