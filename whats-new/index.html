Corrections

When something material changes, such as a regulation, a calculation or a claim that was too strong, it is listed here with the date and the pages affected. Small wording and layout changes are not listed.

  • 26 November 2026: Privacy answers on tool pages corrected: your inputs are kept in your own browser's storage so they are there when you return; they are not sent to Orion Design. Tool pages
  • 26 November 2026: Worked example unit cost corrected from £0.34 to £0.33, and rib thickness guidance made consistent at about 40 to 60 per cent of the wall thickness. Injection moulding explained, Injection moulding DFM checklist
  • 26 November 2026: Corrected an example about confusing margin and markup that overstated the effect on target cost. Route to market and margins
  • 26 November 2026: Added a clear warning to disclose an invention publicly only after filing, and a note that the guides are not legal advice. Design registration vs patents, How to protect product IP
  • 26 November 2026: R&D tax relief: eligibility wording narrowed so it no longer suggests most product development qualifies; the guide points to official guidance and advice. Funding routes in the UK and Northern Ireland
  • 26 November 2026: Import VAT: corrected to say it is normally reclaimable by VAT-registered businesses, so it affects cash flow rather than cost, and no longer counts towards the UK and overseas cost comparison for them. Landed cost: freight, duty and VAT, UK vs overseas manufacturing
  • 26 November 2026: Product safety in Northern Ireland: the guides now say that the EU General Product Safety Regulation has applied in Northern Ireland since 13 December 2024. They previously described the older UK-wide rules as applying everywhere. Product certification basics, IP, partners and compliance

How content is maintained

  • Each guide shows when it was last reviewed, and is reviewed at least every 12 months.
  • Worked examples are labelled as illustrative and their arithmetic is checked.
  • Regulatory points link to official sources such as GOV.UK and legislation, and are overviews, not legal advice.
  • External links are checked automatically every week.

Spotted a mistake? Please get in touch and it will be checked and corrected.
