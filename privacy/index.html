Privacy

Privacy and analytics.

Orion Design uses this site to publish engineering content and tools. Google Analytics 4 and Ahrefs Web Analytics support are built into the site, and no analytics cookies are set without your consent.

What this site currently does

This static site delivers HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and image assets. It includes a consent-aware analytics loader for Google Analytics 4 and Ahrefs Web Analytics. GA4 handles page-view and interaction events such as tool usage, result-copy actions, contact clicks, and the words typed into the site search (so missing guides can be added; anything that looks like an email address or phone number is not sent). The site search itself runs in your browser using an index downloaded from this site; no outside search service is involved. Until you make a choice, GA4 runs in Google Consent Mode with all storage denied: it sets no cookies and sends only anonymous, cookieless measurements that Google uses for aggregate counts. Advertising signals are always denied. Accepting allows GA4 analytics cookies and loads Ahrefs Web Analytics for aggregated web analytics. Declining switches Google Analytics off on this browser and nothing further is sent.

Information kept only in your browser

Some features store information in your own browser's local storage so they work when you come back: tool inputs, your Product Plan, your analytics and currency choices, and the date of your last visit, which is used to show guides and tools added since then. This information is not sent to Orion Design or anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Analytics handling principles

  • Use a consent-first implementation for visitors in relevant jurisdictions.
  • Avoid sending personal or sensitive data to analytics platforms. The figures you enter in the tools stay in your browser (and in the page address when you share a link); the page address reported to analytics leaves them out.
  • Keep the analytics providers and purposes described clearly in this page.

Manage analytics preference

You can reset your local analytics choice on this browser and trigger the consent banner again.
