Questions people ask before getting in touch

Do I need a finished specification or drawings before contacting Orion Design?

No. A short description of the product, the problem it solves, what you have tried so far and what worries you about the next step is enough. Turning that into a clear brief and development plan is part of the work. Working with a product design consultancy explains what a useful brief covers.

Can you help if I am not based in Belfast or Northern Ireland?

Yes. Much product design engineering work happens through CAD reviews, calls and shared prototypes, and clients and manufacturing partners have included businesses in Great Britain, the Republic of Ireland, France, Germany, Sweden, Poland, the United States, China, Taiwan, India, Australia and New Zealand. The MLT Industries case study shows a product taken from concept into manufacture with the client in Australia.

What is the difference between product design and product design engineering?

Product design often focuses on form, user experience and appearance. Product design engineering also covers how the product works and how it will be made: mechanical architecture, materials, tolerances, assembly, manufacturing route and cost. Orion Design works across both, with an emphasis on getting the product ready to manufacture.

How much does product design support cost?

It depends on the decision and the depth of help needed, from a focused review of one question to hands-on development across several stages. Describe where the product is and what you need to decide, and the scope and cost can be agreed before any work begins.

Can I use the free tools before getting in touch?

Yes, and it often makes the first conversation more useful. Save results from the tools to your Product Plan Builder and send the plan along with your question.

Need help with a live product decision?

If you want to test a decision yourself first, the tools are there. If you already have a live question around concept, manufacturability, cost, or launch, send a short note and I can help shape the next step.
